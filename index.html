Model assumptions & formulas

Severity & Limited Expected Loss

L(x) = E[X]Φ(z − σ) + xΦ(−z)

z = (ln x − μ) / σ,   μ = ln(100k) − σ²/2

Fixed $100k ground-up mean across tails; normal CDF via Abramowitz & Stegun (26.2.17).

Layer Mechanics & ILF

ILF = L(b) / L(a),   Excess = L(b) − L(a)

E[Layer | X > a] = (L(b) − L(a)) / P(X > a)

Attachment a sets the ILF base; coverage limit b defines layer width b − a.

Monte Carlo Verification

N = 65,536 draws · seed 2026

MC error ~ O(1 / √N)

Fixed-seed Rust/WASM cross-check; finite-sample error scales as 1/√N and widens at high attachments or heavier tails.

Synthetic model · excludes client data, ALAE, and treaty terms.

Reserving methods & benchmark datasets

Chain Ladder & Mack (1993)

fj = Σ Ci,j+1 / Σ Ci,j

Volume-weighted LDFs project each origin to ultimate; Mack’s distribution-free variance separates process and parameter risk.

BF, Cape Cod & Runoff

IBNRBF = ELR · E · (1 − 1/CDF)

Cape Cod derives ELR from used-up exposure (E / CDF). Cash flow steps along 1/CDF with linear 6-month interpolation; tail beyond 120m groups into 18+ mo.
